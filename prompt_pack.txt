### Materials (3D)
- Liquid mercury: MeshPhysicalMaterial, metalness 1, roughness 0.05-0.15, clearcoat 1, envMap = soft white studio HDRI (light, high-key, NOT a dark environment).

- Holo-glass: MeshTransmissionMaterial (drei) with chromatic aberration, thickness, and an iridescence layer.
- Thin-film iridescence on edges via the iridescence properties of MeshPhysicalMaterial.
- Soft contact shadows on a white ground plane; ambient occlusion (N8AO) so objects feel physically placed in a bright room.

### Typography
- Display: a sharp, wide grotesk or variable sans (e.g. "Space Grotesk", "Unbounded" or "Clash Display"-style; choose an open-licence font and self-host it).

- Mono (for code, metrics, system labels): "JetBrains Mono" or "Geist Mono".

- Small uppercase mono labels as "system annotations" throughout (e.g. "SYS.04 / AGENTIC_LAYER / LATENCY 182ms").

### Motion language

- Smooth scroll (Lenis) synced to GSAP ScrollTrigger and the R3F render loop.
- Magnetic buttons, a custom cursor (a small chrome droplet that stretches with velocity and turns into a lens over interactive items).
